## 🇮🇳 India-Specific Verification Tips

- **`.ac.in` emails** are accepted by Microsoft Azure, JetBrains, Notion, and Microsoft 365 directly.
- **GitHub Student Pack** is the master key — apply with your college ID card photo + `.ac.in` email for best results.
- **SheerID** is used by Figma and Perplexity — it works with most Indian universities.
- **No credit card perks** (16 out of 20) are perfect for students who don't have international payment cards yet.
- If your college email doesn't work, try uploading your **college ID card**, **fee receipt**, or **enrollment letter** as proof.

## ❓ Common Issues & Fixes

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| GitHub Pack rejected | Use `.ac.in` email + upload a clear photo of your physical student ID card with visible dates |
| Azure says "not eligible" | Make sure your `.ac.in` email domain is registered with Microsoft. Contact your college IT department |
| JetBrains won't verify | Try the GitHub Pack verification method instead of email |
| Figma says "not a student" | Use SheerID verification and make sure your university is listed |
| Oracle asks for credit card | A standard Indian debit card (Visa/Mastercard) works — no charges are made |
